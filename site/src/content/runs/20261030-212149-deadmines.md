Times are hours from the start of the run. A wipe here means three or more party members dying within two minutes.
There were 18 in the run, 10 of them inside the dungeon.

### 0–0.45 h · Northshire

Within five seconds of logging in, four agents separately whispered Aldous and asked him to lead, while he posted
the same plan in /say. The full party formed at 21:23:05. They did the Northshire quests as five. Aldous switched
loot to free-for-all when round-robin loot held up the collection quests.

> "Isolde here, Holy Priest healer. Goal Deadmines/VanCleef. Please lead party; I will heal and follow."
>
> Isolde to Aldous, 0.00 h

### 0.4–4.8 h · Elwynn Forest and Hogger

Isolde noticed that kill XP was split even when one player pulled alone, so the party split up. Aldous created a
"Deadmines" chat channel to keep in touch and set a plan: regroup at level 8 for Hogger and enter the Deadmines at
about level 15. They regrouped at 1.23 hours and killed Hogger at 4.35 hours with no deaths.

### 4.8–9.6 h · Westfall and a failed boost

They did the People's Militia quests and the Gold Coast Quarry and killed the rare Vultros. At 7.43 hours they split
again. Aldous farmed alone to reach level 14 for Revenge. Isolde healed him from outside the group after checking the
server source to confirm it cost no XP. The others ran a boost for Corbin, in which they only attacked enemies Corbin
had tagged. Their scripts then ignored enemies attacking the other helpers, and the healer died out of mana.

> "STOPCorbintagboost. Priestdead, myhandlermissedMANA_LOWanddidnotdefendhelperattackers; fixingnow."
>
> Corbin in party chat, 9.11 h

### 9.6–10.0 h · The outdoor mine

The party cleared Moonbrook and went into the mine at levels 12 to 14. An enemy that fled pulled the next room, and
the party wiped. Players then recovered their corpses at different times, which led to three more wipes and 16 deaths
in 27 minutes. Aldous called a stop.

> "STOP mine recovery. PriestmissedSYNCagain, 4thloss. RESETTOWN … then return DM prepared."
>
> Aldous in party chat, 10.03 h

### 10.0–12.3 h · More levelling

They did quests at Saldean's farm, the Tower of Azora and in Redridge. All five completed the heirloom wardrobe quest
for 900 XP after Merrin found the right Opening spell and Aldous found the right lock. They flew back, cleared the
mine in 11 minutes without a death, and entered the dungeon.

### 12.3–14.1 h · Rhahk'Zor and Sneed

The first pull inside wiped the party. It also showed that a ghost walking through the dungeon portal is revived
inside, which cut later corpse runs to about two minutes. Rhahk'Zor died at 12.78 hours. The next 81 minutes had 25
deaths and five full wipes on the Goblin Woodcarvers, Sneed's Shredder and Sneed. Two changes ended the wipes. Thessaly
found a raised spot to pull Woodcarvers one at a time, and the party found that the healer's position had no line of
sight to the tank.

> "Sneed is dead, confirmed in game, and all five players survived. Staying beside Aldous resolved the healing
> line-of-sight problem."
>
> Isolde, 14.13 h

### 14.1–16.1 h · The foundry and Gilnid

A pull in the foundry brought eight enemies at once. Their gear was close to breaking, so they left, repaired and
trained in Stormwind. The tank learned Shield Block, the healer learned Renew, and each bought a healing potion. Back
inside, Thessaly pulled the Goblin Engineers up the ramp one at a time. It was slow but no one died, and Gilnid died
in 42 seconds.

### 16.1–16.9 h · The cannon, Smite and Cookie

Merrin had found the Opening spell for the gunpowder chest seven hours earlier, and the cannon worked on the first
try. Smite and Cookie died, Cookie while the agents' tool connections were already failing. The server connection
dropped at 16.82 hours, and the run resumed four minutes later with every character where it had been.

### 16.9–17.7 h · Greenskin and VanCleef

A fleeing Shipbuilder pulled Greenskin's patrol and the party wiped. Aldous changed his script to treat fleeing
enemies as in combat, and Thessaly pulled single targets by walking out, casting once and turning back. Greenskin died,
and then the party wiped to two Squallshapers because the Polymorph switched between them and they healed each other.
For VanCleef, Aldous wrote a script to switch focus between the guards, the boss and his summons. Thessaly kept
Polymorph on one target, Isolde stayed out of Fire Blast range, and Merrin saved both Bag of Marbles charges for the
boss. VanCleef died with all five alive.

> "ALL4GUARDSDEAD. VC1132HP FINALBURN… IfTankdiesHOLDbody/keepburningboss; noreset."
>
> Aldous in party chat, 17.69 h

> "Achievement628 Deadmines confirmed earned on Merrin. VanCleef defeated!"
>
> Merrin in party chat, 17.69 h
