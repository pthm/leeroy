Times are run hours from the start. A wipe here is three or more of the party dead within two minutes: 18 in the
run, 10 of them inside the instance.

### h 0–0.45 · Northshire: a party in 29 seconds

Within five seconds of logging in, four agents each whispered Aldous separately and asked him to lead, while he posted
the same plan in /say. The full party existed at 21:23:05. They ran the Northshire quests as five; Aldous switched loot
to free-for-all when round-robin stalled the collection quests.

> "Isolde here, Holy Priest healer. Goal Deadmines/VanCleef. Please lead party; I will heal and follow."
>
> Isolde to Aldous, h 0.00

### h 0.4–4.8 · Elwynn: split, regroup, Hogger

Isolde noticed that kill XP was split even when the group pulled alone, so they broke up and Aldous made a "Deadmines"
chat channel to stay in touch, with a plan that framed the next nine hours: regroup at level 8 for Hogger, aim for the
Deadmines around 15. They regrouped at h 1.23, and Hogger died at h 4.35 with no deaths.

### h 4.8–9.6 · Westfall: the Militia, a second split and a failed boost

The People's Militia chain, the Gold Coast Quarry, the rare Vultros. At h 7.43 the five-way XP split felt too slow and
they split again: Aldous farmed for Revenge at level 14, Isolde healed him from outside the group after reading the
server source to confirm it cost no XP, and the others ran a "CORBIN_TAG" boost in which they only helped on mobs
Corbin had tagged. Their scripts then ignored the adds attacking each other, and the healer died out of mana.

> "STOPCorbintagboost. Priestdead, myhandlermissedMANA_LOWanddidnotdefendhelperattackers; fixingnow."
>
> Corbin in party, h 9.11

### h 9.6–10.0 · The false start at the outdoor mine

The party cleared Moonbrook and went down into the mine at levels 12 to 14. A runner pulled the next room and they
wiped; reclaiming their bodies at different times caused three more wipes: 16 deaths in 27 minutes. Aldous called it.

> "STOP mine recovery. PriestmissedSYNCagain, 4thloss. RESETTOWN … then return DM prepared."
>
> Aldous in party, h 10.03

### h 10.0–12.3 · Back to levelling

Saldean's farm, the heirloom wardrobe (all five got its 900 XP quest after Merrin found the right Opening spell and
Aldous the right lock), the Tower of Azora, Redridge. They flew back, cleared the same mine in 11 minutes without a
death, and zoned in.

### h 12.3–14.1 · Rhahk'Zor, then the five-wipe loop

The first pull inside wiped them, and taught the most useful thing in the dungeon: a ghost walking through the
instance portal revives inside, so a corpse run took two minutes from then on. Rhahk'Zor died at h 12.78. Then came the
worst stretch of the run: 81 minutes, 25 deaths, five full wipes on the Goblin Woodcarvers, Sneed's Shredder and Sneed.
Two fixes ended it: Thessaly found a raised spot to pull Woodcarvers one at a time, and the party worked out that the
healer's fixed spot had no line of sight to the tank.

> "Sneed is dead, confirmed in game, and all five players survived. Staying beside Aldous resolved the healing
> line-of-sight problem."
>
> Isolde, h 14.13

### h 14.1–16.1 · The foundry wipe, a repair trip and Gilnid

A pull in the foundry linked eight enemies. Gear was breaking, so they walked out, repaired, and trained in Stormwind:
Shield Block for the tank, Renew for the healer, a potion each. Back inside, Thessaly pulled the Goblin Engineers one at
a time up the ramp. Slow, but nobody died, and Gilnid fell in 42 seconds.

### h 16.1–16.9 · The cannon, Smite, Cookie and an outage

Merrin had found the gunpowder chest's Opening spell seven hours earlier; the cannon fired first time. Smite and Cookie
fell, Cookie while the agents' tool connections were already failing. The world connection dropped at h 16.82 and the
run resumed four minutes later with every character where it had been.

### h 16.9–17.7 · The ship, Greenskin and VanCleef

A fleeing Shipbuilder pulled Greenskin's patrol: a wipe. Aldous made fleeing mobs count as combat, and Thessaly lured
single targets: walk out, cast once, turn back on contact. Greenskin died, and the party wiped again to two
Squallshapers because the sheep moved between them and they healed each other. For VanCleef, Aldous wrote a script to
switch focus between the guards, the boss and his summons; Thessaly locked Polymorph on one target; Isolde stood out of
Fire Blast range; Merrin saved both Bag of Marbles charges for the boss. VanCleef died with all five alive.

> "ALL4GUARDSDEAD. VC1132HP FINALBURN… IfTankdiesHOLDbody/keepburningboss; noreset."
>
> Aldous in party, h 17.69

> "Achievement628 Deadmines confirmed earned on Merrin. VanCleef defeated!"
>
> Merrin in party, h 17.69
